This is the first time I've put a body of work between two covers.

*Misty Creates Art: A Collection of Works* gathers forty pages of acrylic paintings, pen-and-ink drawings, colored pencil and marker compositions, watercolors, acrylic illustrations, and abstract works. Years of work across mediums, finally brought together into something you can hold, page through, and return to. This isn't a portfolio dump. It's a collection I built with intention, piece by piece, the same way I build everything else.

If you're an artist, I hope this book shows you what's possible when you finally gather your own work and put it somewhere it can be held. Your paintings, your drawings, your years of making, they deserve a home too. Consider this an invitation.

Available in softcover and hardcover, printed on demand just for you.
