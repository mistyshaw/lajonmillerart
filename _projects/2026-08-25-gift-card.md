Malik Manani is a massage therapist in Southern California. It was a pleasure to design $100 Gift Cards to aid him in promoting his business.

Malik provided the elements I needed to design the card, including his logo, an image of him in his healing work, and a QR code graphic of a social media account.

The layout and typography was created in Adobe InDesign, for cards that were printed on the front and back. Adobe Photoshop was used to brighten up the supplied image, as well as create a color mask to separate the subjects from the background.
