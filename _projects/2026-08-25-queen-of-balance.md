This regal painting is a favorite on the wall of my living space. The pink hair is inspired by the female-forward art of Long Beach, CA artist <a href="https://www.instagram.com/theartoflajon" target="_blank">LaJon Miller</a>. This painting is also inspired by the Queens of the well known symbology of the traditional Rider-Waite-Smith tarot deck. The Queens in the deck individually represent the four elements of air, fire, water, and earth. I like to think that when one has all of these representations, they are balanced in spirit, hence the title of this painting.
