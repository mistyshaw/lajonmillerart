Some time ago, I participated in the annual <a href="https://inktober.com/" target="_blank">Inktober Challenge</a>, created by artist Jake Parker. It was a way to practice my drawing and inking skills. This page is a visual document of fun I’ve had with this annual challenge. Above this paragraph are a few of my favorite challenges. If you’ve seen the first installment of the 2003 movie Kill Bill, you’d likely remember an an unfortunate situation witnessed by a young girl named Nikkia Bell. This illustration is my imagining of an adult version of Ms. Bell, preparing to avenge in a sequel of this movie, if that were to happen! The details of sketching the keys here were a nice challenge, as well as the yummy stack of waffles. Continue to explore the other drawings on this page!
